What the validators agree on

The model reads the source, the translation and the numbered glossary, and answers which numbers the translation gets wrong or leaves out plus one fluency word. The equivalence principle compares that set of numbers and that word, nothing else, so two validators may explain themselves differently and still agree.

The grade is computed in the contract: no violations and fluent is pass, up to two violations is minor, anything else is fail. A stricter or friendlier reader can change a label, never the count.
